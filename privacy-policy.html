1. Overview & Data Storage

Tasklume is primarily a local-first app. Tasks, notes, profile details, custom categories, settings, and focus history are stored on your device. Tasklume does not operate a user account server or cloud-sync service. Some optional features can contact third-party services as described below.

  • Local storage: App data is stored in local app storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may permanently remove it; use the backup feature to keep a copy.
  • Optional text translation: If you choose the Translate action, the task title and/or description you selected are sent to Google's public translation endpoint to obtain a translation. Avoid translating sensitive content unless you are comfortable sending it to that provider. Translation is optional and is not required to use tasks, notes, reminders, or timers.
  • Speech recognition: When you use voice typing, audio is passed to the speech-recognition provider available on your device. Depending on the device, operating-system settings, and provider, speech recognition may process audio off-device. Tasklume does not run its own speech-recognition server.
  • Voice memos: Recordings are saved as files in the app's local storage unless you choose to share them using another app.

2. Android Device Permissions & Legitimate Use

Tasklume requests only the permissions necessary to provide user-requested functionality:

  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used when you explicitly start voice typing or record a voice memo. Voice memos are saved locally. Voice typing uses the available device speech-recognition provider, whose processing and network behavior depend on the provider and device settings.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver timely notifications for task due dates, reminders, and focus timer countdowns.
  • Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required by Android to trigger countdown alerts and alarms at the exact second scheduled by the user.
  • Boot Receiver (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Automatically reschedules user-configured alarms if your smartphone is restarted.

3. Third-Party Services, Tracking & Advertising

Tasklume does not operate an advertising network, user database, or cloud-sync backend. The optional translation feature sends selected task text to Google's translation endpoint, and voice typing may use the operating system's configured speech-recognition provider. These services are controlled by their respective providers and may apply their own terms and privacy practices. Tasklume does not sell your personal information.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Tasklume is not designed to collect children's personal data through a Tasklume-operated account service. Because optional translation and speech-recognition features use third-party providers, guardians should review those providers' applicable terms and privacy practices before allowing children to use these features.
