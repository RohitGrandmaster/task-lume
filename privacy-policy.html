1. Overview & Data Storage

We respect and protect your privacy. TodoMaster operates entirely offline on your device:

  • 100% Local Storage: All your tasks, subtasks, notes, voice memo text, and custom settings are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
  • No Server Transmission: We do not operate external database servers that upload or retain your personal information.

2. Android Device Permissions & Legitimate Use

TodoMaster requests only the permissions necessary to provide user-requested functionality:

  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely for on-device real-time speech-to-text typing when you tap the voice button. Audio data is processed in real time and is NEVER saved, uploaded, or transmitted.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver timely notifications for task due dates, reminders, and focus timer countdowns.
  • Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required by Android to trigger countdown alerts and alarms at the exact second scheduled by the user.
  • Boot Receiver (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Automatically reschedules user-configured alarms if your smartphone is restarted.

3. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

TodoMaster does not include any third-party behavioral analytics, ad networks, or data-broker SDKs. Your personal information is never sold, shared, rented, or monetized.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TodoMaster does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. It complies with COPPA and Google Play Developer Program policies.
